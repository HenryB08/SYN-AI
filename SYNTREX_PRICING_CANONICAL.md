## THE COST FLOOR

Per Growth client, monthly running cost (approximate):

- AI inference (Claude Haiku, ~5,000 msgs, capped): ~$5.00
- Twilio phone number (US local): $1.15
- A2P campaign fee (shared across clients): ~$1.00
- SMS segments (~500/mo): ~$5.50
- Email follow-up (pooled): ~$1.00
- Cloudflare / D1 / hosting (pooled): ~$0.50

**True cost per Growth client: ~$14/month.**

One-time install carries a real per-client A2P sub-brand registration fee (low
tens of dollars) plus setup labor. The install fee covers this; it is not padding.

**The guarantee (applies to all Growth plans):**
If the Growth System captures no value in the client's first month, that month is
free. Stated plainly, in writing: no captured value, free month. The Receipt is
the proof; its numbers must be defensible by hand against the raw event data.

Margins: Core ~96%, Pro ~92%.

Pricing note: $349/$549/$497 are the tested entry numbers. Test against the first
cohort. If they say yes without flinching, there was room higher — raise it.
Never guess the ceiling; let the cohort find it.

## THE RULE THAT MAKES IT COHERENT

- **Growth** is the cold-market machine: printed, guaranteed, runs on SYN's rails,
  priced to a stranger who has never met you.
- **Presence** and **Brand** are warm-market: custom-quoted, sold to people who
  already trust you, priced up from a floor.
- Never cold-pitch a website. Never custom-quote the Growth System.
- The three do not compete. They are three doors into the same company at three
  levels of relationship.
